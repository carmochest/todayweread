About us

A bookshop where creativity and curiosity meet

Today We Read is a tightly curated bookshop designed for discovery and delight. Every corner celebrates design, storytelling and cross-generational curiosity. Our monthly mini-exhibitions, curated tables and workshops invite readers to explore, learn and play.

Curation with a point of viewFewer books, chosen harder. Every title earns its shelf.
Design-first thinkingObjects, covers and spaces that reward a second look.
Guaranteed enjoymentIf a book doesn't delight, it doesn't go on the table.
Cross-generational curiosityPicture books for adults, essays for eleven-year-olds.

Exchange & refund policy

We accept returns only if 1) you receive the wrong item, or 2) the item was damaged during delivery. If the item received is not the correct order or is defective, email us so we can arrange an exchange or refund.

  • Exchanges — within 7 business days from the date of the tax receipt; the new item is resent within 14 business days.
  • Refunds by credit card — requested before dispatch: refunded to the card within 30 business days; requested after dispatch: refunded within 30 days after the product is returned.
  • Refunds by other methods — refunded to your savings account within 30 days after the product is returned.
  • Exchanges and refunds are accepted within 7 business days from the tax receipt date; please keep the receipt. No refund after 7 business days.
  • If stock is on hand we exchange; otherwise we resend within 30 business days, or you may request a refund instead.
  • Customer service: contact@todayweread.com · 10:00am–6:00pm every day.
